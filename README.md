Xubify
All-in-one multimedia hub for downloading, organizing, playing and converting music and videos.
💙 Xubify

Your media. Your way.

**Xubify** is an all-in-one multimedia hub designed to bring your music, videos and media collection together in one place.

Download, organise, play, convert and personalise your media through one convenient desktop application. Whether you're managing your music library, creating playlists, converting audio or video, or enjoying your favourite media, Xubify gives you the tools to make your media experience your own.

---

✨ Features

- **Media Downloader** – Download supported media and save it to your local collection.
- **Built-in Media Player** – Play your music and videos without needing a separate player.
- **Music & Video Playlists** – Create and manage separate playlists for your favourite music and videos.
- **Media Library** – Keep your downloaded and imported media organised in one place.
- **Recently Downloaded** – Quickly access your latest downloads without searching through your entire library.
- **Audio Converter** – Convert audio files between supported formats.
- **Video Converter** – Convert video files into supported formats.
- **Audio Extraction** – Extract audio from supported video files.
- **Batch Conversion** – Process multiple media files in one operation.
- **Download History** – Keep track of your previous downloads.

*Features and supported formats may vary between releases.*

🎨 Designed Around Your Media

Xubify combines a nostalgic multimedia-player feel with a fresh, modern interface inspired by the glossy aesthetics of the 2000s.

Its goal is simple: make managing and enjoying your personal media collection easier, more convenient and more personal.
